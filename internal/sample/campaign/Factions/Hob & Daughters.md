# Hob & Daughters

*A salvage consortium, eleven boats, one winch-run, and a majority stake in every
structure between here and the hills.*

## What they are

Not a guild and not a cartel. A family business with the family expanded twice:
Hob [[Hob Copperhew]], his elder daughter Marda, his younger daughter Tansy, and
eleven flat-bottomed hulls that go out on the autumn tide and come back loaded
with eel, reed, shell and silt.

They own the causeway winch-run. Not the road — the road is a Compact structure
that all nine cities are notionally responsible for and that nobody has
maintained since the breaking — but the **winch-run**, which is the business of
getting a flat-bottomed hull over the mud at low water, and which is not
something you can improvise.

That is the whole of their leverage and it is enormous.

## What they want

**The road to open.** Not for ideology, not for the Compact, not for anyone
else's history. Because the road opening is the forty days a year that eleven
boats have a reason to exist, and without it Greywake is a place people used to
live.

Hob is entirely honest about this. He will tell the party on the first meeting,
unprompted, in about four sentences, and he will not dress it up, and he will
then decline to discuss it further.

## The complication

They have been buying Compact clause-pieces for nine years, for parts, from the
other eight cities, and they have no idea what they are doing. Marda has three
of them in a strongbox in the Kettle cellar and has never had them read. Two of
them are genuine clauses from the Greywake copy. The third is not.

Marda is a practical woman and has decided the strongbox is a problem and not
acted on it. This is the reason [[Dame Alis Rennard]] rents the cellar, and Alis
has been in that cellar exactly twice in four years and knows the count and not
the contents.

> [!warning] The letters
> Hob has been sending a letter to the Bellhouse for four years. He has sent
> four couriers and three came back. He does not know that the fourth is running
> the tideline at [[Hollowfen]], and the fourth is not a courier any more and
> will not be talked into being one.

## How to play the family

- **Marda** does the arithmetic and is better at it than her father, and is
  aware that being better at it is not the same as being in charge.
- **Tansy** runs the winch-run, is thirty-one, and is the one who has looked at
  the third clause-purchase and asked what it was for.
- **Hob** is the front, is entirely comfortable being the front, and has one
  unrepeated lie in him that the game master should save until the party has
  earned it.

## For the game master

This faction is the reason the campaign has a *clock* rather than a countdown.
They are not going to harm the party, they are going to keep asking the party to
hurry, and hurry is the pressure the whole campaign runs on.

They are also the party with the most to lose from a dissolution and the least
idea of what the dissolution is, which makes them the natural place for a player
to discover the actual shape of the problem by accident.
