# Dame Alis Rennard

*Human, 52. Captain of the [[Greywake]] fen-watch. Twenty-six years in it,
eleven of them as captain. Has arrested forty-one people for being in the wrong
place.*

## Who she is

Alis enforces the nine-year silence. That is her job: the fen-watch's actual
mandate is tidal, and the actual work is the *quiet*, and the quiet is a thing
the Assembly pushed for in the year after the breaking and the fen agreed to
because the alternative was an argument.

She is good at it. She has never once been cruel about it and she has never once
let it stop her.

## What she wants

She wants the third bell to be explained. Not punished, not prevented —
*explained*. She has had a file on it for nine years, and the file is a list of
forty-one names and a question mark, and the question mark is the only thing
about Alis Rennard that is not a matter of record.

The third bell is the bell that rang on the night of the breaking and had no
cause anyone can name. Alis has the only surviving witness and the witness will
not talk and Alis has never asked her, because asking is an admission that the
file was wrong.

## How to play her

- Says the time, the date, and the number of people present, in that order, and
  always in that order. It is a discipline, not a tic.
- Will not sit with her back to a door. Will not notice that she does it.
- Refers to [[Nettle Ninefingers]] as *the child* and means it kindly.
- Under real pressure, does the arithmetic: names, arrests, files, procedure.
  It is her best quality and it is what will lose her.

## For the game master

Alis is the campaign's clock made of a person. She is the reason the party gets
*four days* rather than *some time*, and the deadline is not arbitrary: the
fen-watch's own quiet is due for renewal at the start of the tide, and if the
party is still on the causeway when the lotteries are drawn, the whole
Greywake arrangement becomes a thing under investigation rather than a
continuity.

She is also the one who can be turned. Not bought — Alis cannot be bought,
there is nothing in [[Greywake]] she wants. *Convinced.* The party can convince
her in session four by producing a document that makes the silence
indefensible, and at that point she will become the most dangerous ally in the
campaign, because she will act on it immediately and there will be no time to
think.
