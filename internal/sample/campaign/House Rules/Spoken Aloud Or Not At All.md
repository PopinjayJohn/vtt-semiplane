# Spoken Aloud Or Not At All

*Agreed at table. Components are unchanged; the volume is not.*

## The rule

> **A verbal component is a sound, not an item.** It carries thirty feet, to
> anyone awake who is listening. There is no silent casting.

The rule as written is below, in the fence. It is a `table` secret, which
means every signed-in reader at the table can read it — see
[[Writing secrets]].

```secret id=e75da40db8f6 visibility=table author=dungeonmaster created=2026-01-24T19:10:00Z title="Verbal, at thirty feet"
A spell with a verbal component is audible to every creature within thirty feet who is
awake and has a reason to be listening.

There is no silent casting. Somatic and material components are silent and
unchanged. A caster who wants to be quiet has three honest options: a *Silence*
spell, a *Message* or *Minor Illusion* cover, or a bow.

This is not used to ambush the party. If they are seen casting, they are seen
casting; the point is the decision, not the gotcha.
```

## Why

The published rules make components a resource-management question: a character
with a component pouch is more mobile than one without, and a *silent cast*
widely used at a table is a homebrew nobody voted on.

Making the component a *sound* rather than an item moves the question from "what
have you brought" to "who is listening", which is a question the campaign is
already asking. [[Nettle Ninefingers]] listens for a living. [[Dame Alis Rennard]] has two hundred yards of causeway and forty-one names. A caster who
lights up the road is making a decision about the whole party, and that is a
scene worth having.

## In practice

- Thirty feet, awake, listening. A sleeping creature is not woken; an awake
  creature that is *not* looking in that direction is not alerted, but it has
  heard something and will investigate.
- **Somatic and material components are silent and unchanged.** This rule is
  only about the voice.
- A caster who wants to be quiet has three honest options: a *Silence* spell,
  a *Message* or *Minor Illusion* cover, or a bow. All three are already in the
  rules.
- Do not use this to ambush the party. If they get seen casting, they get seen
  casting — the point is the decision, not the gotcha.
