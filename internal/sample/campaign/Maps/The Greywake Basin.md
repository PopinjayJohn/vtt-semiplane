# The Greywake Basin

*The whole campaign in one picture. Eleven miles, one road, one island, one
chapterhouse in the water.*

## Distances

| From | To | On foot | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Greywake]] | the [[Salt Road]]'s first waymarker | 1 hour | Mud from the gate out |
| the 8th waymarker | [[Hollowfen]] gate | 2 days | The last four miles are uphill and the road stops being flat |
| the Hollowfen gate | the Bellhouse | 3 hours | Crossable only at low water, twice a day, four hours each |
| [[Greywake]] | anywhere west | — | Nine years of not going |

## The three things on the chart that matter

**The road is a line.** Everything in the campaign happens on a flat causeway
eleven miles long, and there is nowhere to hide on it. The [[Salt Road Ambush]]
is on the road precisely because the road is the only place the party is
predictable.

**The Bellhouse is off the road, at the end of a creek.** Four hours a day at low
water. This is the campaign's clock made of geography, and it is why the party
cannot simply go and get the document in session two.

**The eighth waymarker has no bell.** Four miles from [[Greywake]], mid-road, and
the one thing on this chart that nine years of silence is visibly organised
around. See [[Session Zero - The Wetting]].

## For the game master

Print this and put it on the table. It is a small campaign and the geography is
itself a character: a line, a dot, and a building in the water. When a player
asks "can we get there in time", the answer is on the map, and it is "yes, twice
a day, for four hours, and that is all the time there is".
