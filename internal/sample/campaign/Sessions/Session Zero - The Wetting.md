# Session Zero — The Wetting

*Played as a prequel session: the fen-watch on the last night of the Compact.
This is the log the watch house kept, and it is the only surviving document
written by people who were awake.*

> [!note] What session zero is for
> Before the party ever walks down the [[Salt Road]], this document exists. It is
> the third bell, the missing waymarker, and the whole of the fen-watch's
> founding story, in the flat register of men writing down what they did. It is
> a legal document, a session log, and the best handout in the campaign.
>
> If the group wants a cold open, this is it. If not, the party can find it in
> the watch-house records in session two, and the effect is better.

## 18:04 — Tide high, road closed

Normal. Twenty-two bells, all present, checked by Pell the waymarker-man who is
not a member of the watch and has never been and answers to nobody.

## 21:00 — The third bell

The eighth waymarker rings. It is four miles out. Every bell on the road is
maintained by a levy and all twenty-two are checked twice daily; it is not a
bell that rings on its own.

Watch-captain **Alis Rennard** takes four men and goes out at a run. They are
back at 22:51.

## 22:51 — Report of the watch captain

Recorded by her, in her hand, at the watch-house desk, and never amended:

> Four of us to the eighth waymarker. Bell had been struck three times at
> intervals and was not ringing when we arrived. Cairn intact, no marks, no
> footprints on the flats either side except ours and the tide's.
>
> We took the bell down and carried it in. I did not order this. I found the
> four of us standing round it for a quarter of an hour and I said *take it
> down* and nobody argued, and I have thought about that for nine years and I
> still do not know what I ordered.
>
> Waymarker number eight has not carried a bell since.

## 23:20 — The crown

The Compact crown is broken in the chapterhouse at [[Hollowfen]], in the
presence of the signatories' deputies and the archivist, and this is *not* in
the watch's hands and *not* in this log, and it is here because the watch was
four miles away at 22:51 and four miles is four miles.

What is in this log is the times.

## 23:20 — What arrived afterwards

Eleven people came down the road off the high ground between 23:20 and 02:00,
all of them soaked, none of them able to say where they had been. Names and
descriptions taken from all eleven. All eleven released at dawn. No arrest, no
charge, no finding, because there was nothing to charge them with: they were
standing on a public road in the rain.

The log records that all eleven declined to give an account.

## The file

[[Dame Alis Rennard]] has kept the whole thing. Forty-one names in nine years,
none of them charged, and one question mark in the margin in her own hand
beside the phrase *all eleven declined to give an account*.

She has never asked. Asking would be an admission that the file is a record of
something other than what it says it is.

## Notes for the game master

- **The eleven** are not the party, not the fifth city, and not the Assembly.
  They are nine years old now, they are scattered, and four of them are still in
  the fen. Two are available as characters if the table wants to go looking.
- **The third bell was rung from the road, not from the building.** The party
  will assume the opposite for two sessions, and the assumption is Nettle's,
  and the road is the *honest* source.
- **The bell exists.** It is in the watch-house strongbox in [[Greywake]] and
  Alis has never once looked at it. If a player asks to see it, hand it over;
  it is a bronze bell with a crack in it and nothing supernatural about it at
  all, which is exactly why it has bothered her for nine years.
- The whole of the third-bell thread is on [[The Ninefold Oath]]. The reading
  of the ledger that it forces is on [[The Tide-Clerk Ledger]].
