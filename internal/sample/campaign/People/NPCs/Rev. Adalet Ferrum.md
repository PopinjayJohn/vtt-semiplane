# Rev. Adalet Ferrum

*Human, 71. Warden of the Grey House, [[Greywake]]. Thirty-nine years in the
[[The Quiet Assembly]]. Plays the fen beautifully.*

## Who she is

Adalet is the Quiet Assembly's senior warden in Greywake and the reason the order
is a political force in the fen at all. She has spent her career doing the thing
the Assembly does best: making a moral position sound like housekeeping.

She believes the Compact should be dissolved. She believes this completely, she
believes it is *kindness* rather than cruelty, and she is correct about almost
every part of it. The dissolution ends an obligation that has held two cities
hostage for nine years. It also ends the only mechanism anyone has for opening
the road, and Adalet knows that, and she has decided that ending it is worth it,
and she has not told anybody how heavy the decision was.

## What she wants

The third clause, delivered, and a witness to the dissolution. She does not want
the party's help and will say so. She wants the party's *witness*, and she is
willing to be extremely generous to secure it.

## How to play her

- Never raises her voice and never uses a threat. She has never needed to; she
  says what would be reasonable and waits.
- Refers to the Compact as "the arrangement" and to the crown as "the instrument",
  both of which are her own coinages and both of which make the thing sound
  smaller.
- Is genuinely kind to [[Kess Varro]], and this is the most dangerous thing about
  her. She has noticed that the tide-clerk is the whole argument and she has
  started, carefully, to make friends.
- Lends books. Brings the tea. Sits down uninvited. The Quiet Assembly's whole
  method is hospitality and it is *extremely* effective.

## For the game master

Adalet is the campaign's moral centre and the party will be tempted to hate her
by session three, correctly identified as a false position. Do not rescue her.
The gift in session three is genuinely fair, the cost of it falls entirely on
[[Kess Varro]], and the table gets to notice both of those at the same moment.

## What she decided in the spring

Adalet made the decision in March, alone, in a letter to the Assembly's
provincial house that took four drafts. The reasoning, in the order she had it:

1. **The Compact cannot be honoured and cannot be discharged**, and a thing that
   cannot be honoured is a lie told slowly. The Assembly's position is that nine
   years of silence is not a suspension of a contract, it is a breach of one,
   and a breach discharges the obligor. Theologically sound inside the order;
   legally unprecedented outside it. Adalet knows the difference and does not
   think the difference matters.
2. **She asked whether the dissolution could fall back on the road.** She asked
   [[Ibb Oth-Kerrin]] twice and he said no both times, and then he said *"you
   have asked me twice, Warden, which is the answer."* She has not forgiven him
   for it and cannot do without him.
3. **She wrote to the sixth city** asking them to accept the road's loss in
   exchange for the release of two people held under the Compact's guarantee of
   passage. That city did not reply. The two are still held. They are nineteen
   and twenty-one, and they are the reason the Assembly is not simply a villain,
   and the party does not learn about them until session four.
4. **Her fourth draft, which she did not send**, offers the road to the fen for
   one more Compact cycle in exchange for signing the dissolution. It is the
   right offer. It requires the fen to trust that a Compact cycle means
   something after dissolution, which it does not, and the fen knows it does
   not, and that knowledge is the actual reason this campaign is hard.

> [!danger] For the game master
> The payload for the party is item three. They can find the two in session four
> by accident, by following [[Nettle Ninefingers]]'s suppliers. Adalet will not
> have told them, will not deny it, and will not say it first.
