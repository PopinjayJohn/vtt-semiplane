# Ibb Oth-Kerrin

*Human, 84. Last archivist of the Greywake Compact. Very nearly blind. Has
answered the question "is the document still there?" truthfully, twice.*

## Who he is

Ibb was the Compact's archivist. He had the run of the archive in the Bellhouse,
which means he had the run of the only copy of the Compact's text in existence,
and he was there on the night it broke, and he came out of it.

He came out of it with his hands empty. Everyone assumed the archive went into
the water. He let them assume it, for nine years, because correcting them would
have required explaining how he was in a room full of iron and came out.

## What he wants

He wants one thing and has wanted it for nine years: for somebody to go and get
the document. He will not go himself. He is eighty-four, he is nearly blind, and
the Bellhouse is four hours into water that is chest-deep in a good year.

He cannot ask, because the asking is the confession, so he does the next thing
instead, which is answer questions *honestly and slowly* and hope somebody
notices.

## How to play her — play him

- Answers exactly the question asked, and then volunteers one more thing, and
  that one more thing is always the important one.
- Calls everyone "young" and means it as a form of address rather than an
  insult.
- Has a perfect memory for anything he read before his sight went and none at
  all for anything after, and this is a real disability, not a mysterious one.
- Fears the water absolutely and has been living four hours from it for nine
  years.

## For the game master

Ibb is the campaign's evidence. Everything the party learns about the Compact
that is not hearsay comes from him, and every fact he gives them is true.

The trap is that he has *also* said the true thing that makes him look like a
liar, and he knows he has, and he will not pre-empt it. Let the party work out
which of his statements is the load-bearing one. In session two, the answer is:

**The archive did not go into the water. The archive is under the water.**

The document is intact. It is in the Bellhouse, on the third gallery, in the
reading room, in a copper case that was sealed against the flood. He did not
lose it. He could not carry it out, and he has spent nine years being the man
who did not try hard enough, and the man who did not try hard enough is sitting
in a chair in [[Greywake]] waiting for four people to go and get it.
