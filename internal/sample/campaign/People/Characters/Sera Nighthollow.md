# Sera Nighthollow

*Elf, 112. Came to Greywake by sea, has been in Greywake four months, has not
left.*

## At a glance

Sera is a scholar of the Compact and she has already published, in a city of
eleven thousand people, a treatise arguing that it is a legal fiction. The
treatise is careful, sourced, and completely wrong in a way that will not become
obvious until session two.

She came to prove a negative: that the crown was metal, that the Compact was
paper, that nine years of mutual silence was nine years of people being polite
to each other. She is competent, courteous, and she has misread the evidence
because she misread the one witness who mattered.

She likes this table. She is not yet suspicious of it, which is a state she finds
uncomfortable and has not examined.

## What she wants

A **citation that fails**. She needs to break one clause in public, cleanly, in
front of witnesses, with a document. She has four candidates and two witnesses
and she is prepared to pay for a courier she does not trust, which is the first
time in her life she has been prepared to pay for anything.

## How to play her

- Sera cites. When she is about to do something reckless she cites a source, and
  the citation is real, and it is about a different subject.
- She answers questions with questions and does not notice that she is doing it.
- She is squeamish about physical violence and articulate about everything else,
  which reads as courage until it does not.
- She is 112 and has never once in her life been in a hurry, and she is now in a
  hurry, and this is the most alarming thing about her.

## Notes for the game master

Sera's treatise is the campaign's best red herring and its worst mistake. Her
central error is assuming the Compact was a *written* document that was
*destroyed*. The document is intact. It is under water in the Bellhouse with
everything else, and it has been intact the entire time, and the reason nobody
has read it in nine years is that the Bellhouse is under water, not that the
paper is gone.

Let this land in session two, at [[Ibb Oth-Kerrin]]'s hands, in a way that does
not make Sera stupid — the archive survived; the *readers* did not survive the
road. She will defend the distinction and she will be right to, and the player
will still feel the ground move.

> [!warning] Sera and the Assembly
> Rev. [[Adalet Ferrum]] will offer to *fund* the treatise's publication in
> session three. Sera should take the money. It is the correct decision, the
> Assembly wants the Compact dissolved, and Sera is the perfect instrument for
> it. Let the player find that out after the fact.
