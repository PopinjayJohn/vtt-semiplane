# The Salt Road

*Four hundred yards of mud and packed shell between [[Greywake]] and the
high ground. A road for forty days a year.*

## What it is

The causeway is a raised shell-road laid by the Compact in the year of its
signing, running eleven miles from Greywake's tide-lock to the hills at
[[Hollowfen]] where the ground stops arguing with the sea. It is the only route
in or out of the fen for a loaded cart.

It is a good road. That is the tragedy of it: the Compact built it *well*, and
it is still doing exactly what it was built to do, and it is doing it for a
contract that no longer exists.

## The forty days

The autumn tide raises the delta about a foot and a half, which is enough to
float a flat-bottomed hull over the mud and not enough to bother anything else.
The road opens. Greywake's boats go out. The other eight cities send their own,
for their own reasons, and the whole fen spends a month being somewhere.

The lottery that decides who goes first is the **tide-lottery**, and it is
[[Kess Varro]]'s, and the tide-lottery is governed by the third clause.

## The ambush

Something is on the road. Not one of the eight cities — see
[[The Salt Road Ambush]] for what it is and what it wants.

> [!danger] The road is eleven miles and takes two days by cart
> The party will be walking. That is a deliberate choice: on foot, a road this
> flat is a place where nothing can be seen until it is close, and eleven miles
> of flat nothing is the campaign's best pacing device and costs you nothing to
> run.

## What the road looks like

Shell, packed hard, a raised crown in the middle two feet above the flats, and
a **waymarker every half-mile**: a low cairn with a bronze bell on it, unmelted,
unrusted, maintained by a levy the Compact collected and the fen kept paying out
of habit.

Eight bells. Eleven miles, so twenty-two, plus the two at the ends. The eighth
one from Greywake has no bell on it. It has been empty for nine years.

Nobody in Greywake talks about which one.

> [!note] For the game master
> That is the whole of the road's secret, and the party will find it in session
> one or two without help. Do not point at it. When they mention it, [[Kess Varro]] goes quiet, which is the tell, and which she will not explain.
